# Work collections and service pricing

## Scope and mode

Experience: reorganize the existing portfolio into five discipline collections. Persuade/Read: show scoped service fees without cluttering the homepage. This extends the existing visual world; it is not a redesign or an open concept exercise.

The user confirmed that public editing prices cover editing only. Posting on Furreal accounts, paid-media usage and exclusivity are quoted separately. All existing case studies, media and direct project destinations remain available.

## Direction contract

THESIS: visitors choose a discipline before opening individual work. Replace the ten-card homepage wall with five image-led collection folders; keep the full case studies one step inside.

OWN-WORLD: inherit the existing paper, ink, slate accent, Segoe UI typography, rounded work imagery and editorial rhythm. Real project imagery carries the collections. No new fonts, illustration system or decorative effects.

STORY: identify Moses, open a collection, inspect real work, then find an appropriate service and request a scoped quote. Fees and quote boundaries are readable without opening a PDF.

FIRST VIEWPORT: preserve the homepage hero. The work section leads with collection covers, names and derived counts. Collection routes put the selected discipline above the existing project cards. Services opens with a short fee summary and links to motion, editing and web rates, with detailed scopes progressively disclosed.

FORM: precisely requested folder organization, implemented directly as native links and crawlable collection routes. No direction seed applies to this bounded extension. A folder tab lifts slightly on hover-capable devices; focus is visible and reduced motion stays static. Native details disclose long price lists without extra JavaScript.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Evidence and constraints

- Motion NGN/USD fees and 50/50 terms come from the supplied general invoices.
- Web fees, care and completion-payment terms come from BTI's general guide, not its discounted client invoice.
- New editing rates are authored recommendations, not historical invoices or market averages. No automatic FX conversion, taxes, engagement outcomes or guaranteed delivery dates.
- Public PDFs contain reusable placeholders, not client bills or bank/PayPal accounts. Originals stay untouched outside the repo.
- Preserve existing design authority and reduced-motion behavior. Deploy through the existing hosting project if authenticated access is available; never create a replacement project or buy a plan.

## Portfolio / hiring research

### Nielsen Norman Group — 5 Steps to Creating a UX-Design Portfolio
https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ux-design-portfolios/

NN/g surveyed 204 UX professionals involved in hiring and emphasizes:

- communicating process and value;
- showing role/constraints;
- scannability;
- curation;
- real work;
- case studies that explain more than final screenshots.

Although this portfolio is broader than UX design, the hiring-manager scanning principles are useful for developer/creative project presentation.

### Nielsen Norman Group — UX Hiring: Insights from a Design Recruiter
https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ux-hiring-insights/

Key lesson used:
the portfolio's real user may be a recruiter, PM, founder, or manager rather than another specialist. The presentation should make professional relevance easy to understand without requiring expert interpretation.

### NN/g — UX Portfolios: What Hiring Managers Look For
Available through NN/g's portfolio/career research:
https://www.nngroup.com/articles/growing-your-ux-career-study-guide/

Useful principle:
portfolio expectations depend on audience and seniority; the portfolio should communicate evidence and story efficiently.

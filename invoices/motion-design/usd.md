# Motion design: USD

Existing general invoice rates

15-30 second product launches, app showcases, and animated interface films.

| Service | Scope | Fee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Startup product showcase | One 15-30 second film in one agreed format. | $500 - $900 |
| Established company showcase | A product or brand story with a more involved creative scope. | $800 - $1,500 |
| Campaign / multiple deliverables | A coordinated set of films or campaign assets, scoped together. | $2,000 - $5,000+ |

## Included
Creative direction, animation, timing, sound design, one finished MP4 in one agreed format, and two consolidated revision rounds.

## Separate scope
Additional formats, voiceover, bespoke illustration or 3D, further revisions, rush work, and editable source files are quoted separately.

## Payment
50% upfront and 50% before final delivery.

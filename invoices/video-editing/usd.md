# Video editing: USD

New indicative starting rates

Editing supplied footage for music, brands, personal projects, and social content. No posting is included.

| Service | Scope | Fee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personal / cinematic edit | One 15-30 second edit from up to 5 minutes of supplied footage; pacing, colour, transitions, and sound. | $75 |
| Song promotion edit | One 15-45 second music-led edit using supplied, cleared audio and footage; beat-led cuts and text treatment. | $150 |
| Brand collaboration edit | One 15-60 second branded video from supplied footage, with captions, logo treatment, colour, and sound. | $300 |
| Social / talking-head clip | One clip up to 60 seconds from up to 10 minutes of footage, with cleanup, captions, and light B-roll. | $100 |
| Long-form / YouTube edit | One finished video up to 10 minutes from up to 30 minutes of supplied footage; editing, colour, and audio cleanup. | $300 |

## Included
One finished MP4 in one agreed aspect ratio and two consolidated revision rounds within the agreed brief. Delivery timing is agreed before work starts.

## Separate scope
Filming, advanced VFX/3D, extra versions, source files, rush work, and additional revisions are separate. You supply footage, music, brand assets, and any needed permissions.

## Payment
Proposed editing terms: 50% upfront and 50% before final delivery. The issued project quote confirms the final terms.

Posting on Furreal accounts, paid-ad usage, licensing, exclusivity, and campaign management are quoted separately. No views, engagement, or campaign results are guaranteed.

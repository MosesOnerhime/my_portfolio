## Included
Responsive design, agreed pages/workflows, basic metadata and search setup, testing, deployment assistance, Git source handover, one admin handover session, and two consolidated revision rounds. Includes 30 days of fixes for defects in the agreed functionality after launch.

## Separate scope
Domain, hosting, database, email/SMS, plugins, stock licences, gateway fees and usage overages are separate provider costs. New features, redesigns, third-party outages and unlimited content changes are not included.

## Payment
One full payment when the agreed website is complete, tested and approved by the client. No deposit or development instalments. Source handover follows settlement. Quotes are valid for 14 days.
